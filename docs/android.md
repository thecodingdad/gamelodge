# Android

## Home screen

**Settings → Launcher mode** makes GameLodge the home app: the Home button and the device start lead to
it. You can switch back at any time; GameLodge also works as a normal app.

## Shortcuts in every app

Button shortcuts work in GameLodge and in the stream right away. For other apps – Android games, a browser,
an emulator – switch on **GameLodge combos** in the accessibility settings (**Settings → Controllers →
System-wide**). The service only reads controller buttons, nothing on the screen.

!!! note "Installed outside an app store"
    Android greys the service out until restricted settings are allowed: open GameLodge's app info, menu
    (⋮) → **Allow restricted settings**, then enable the service.

## Permissions

| Permission | Used for |
|---|---|
| Usage access | Play time of apps and games started outside GameLodge |
| Display over other apps | The quick menu over games and streams |
| Modify system settings | Brightness in the quick menu |
| Nearby devices / location | Bluetooth controllers and the Wi-Fi name in quick access |
| Notifications | Status notifications (e.g. while the VPN is connected) |

## Emulators

**Settings → Emulators**: add your ROM folders (one subfolder per system like `snes`, `psx` or `switch` is
recognised) and GameLodge picks an installed emulator per platform. Start commands come from
[ES-DE](https://es-de.org); emulators not confirmed yet are marked experimental, and you can add your own
start command for emulators the list doesn't know. Optional **access to all files** reads ROM folders
faster and finds ES-DE covers.

## Device controls

The quick menu's quick access tab switches Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, airplane mode and the VPN and sets volume and
brightness. On the AYN Odin, the TV output mode is in the Odin settings ("Video output mode").
