# Windows

On Windows GameLodge is a full-screen launcher for your PC games and a streaming client – and the PC can
share its games with your other GameLodge devices.

## Window & startup

**Settings → Window & startup**: full screen, start with Windows, keep running in the tray. A keyboard
shortcut opens the quick menu system-wide, and the Xbox Game Bar gets a GameLodge button.

## Game sources

**Settings → Game sources** scans the stores installed on the PC: Steam, Epic, GOG, Xbox, EA, Ubisoft,
Battle.net, Amazon and Playnite. Owned but not installed games come from the stores' local data (no store
logins). Add your own games and emulators by hand.

## Streaming

The built-in [Moonlight](https://github.com/thecodingdad/moonlight-qt) streams from your other PCs, with
the same hosts, profiles, shortcuts and quick menu as on Android.

## This PC as a streaming host

If the PC runs Sunshine, Apollo or Vibepollo, GameLodge can be its **host companion**:

- **Settings → Streaming host** puts the PC's games into Vibepollo / Apollo / Sunshine as apps.
- **Settings → Devices & pairing** pairs your handhelds and tablets with this PC. Paired devices get the
  PC's games with their artwork and metadata, share play time, ratings, favourites and collections, keep
  the Guide button on the device while streaming, and bring the game's window to the front when they
  resume.
- A web admin page manages paired devices from a browser.

Only paired devices get any access; pairing needs a PIN confirmed on the PC.

!!! tip "Keep GameLodge running"
    Host features need GameLodge to run on the PC – it can stay in the tray.
