# Your PC as host

The Windows app turns the PC you stream from into a **GameLodge host**. Two parts, each optional:

| Page | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Streaming host](streaming-host.md) | Puts the PC's games on Vibepollo, Apollo or Sunshine as apps – for every Moonlight client |
| [Devices & web interface](sharing.md) | Pairs your other GameLodge devices with the PC: games with artwork, shared play data, metadata through the PC's keys |

!!! note "Windows only"
    Both need GameLodge for Windows on the PC that streams, running in the tray.

## How it fits together

1. **Vibepollo / Apollo / Sunshine** on the PC does the streaming.
2. **GameLodge on the PC** fills it with the PC's games ([Streaming host](streaming-host.md)) and shares their
   data with paired devices ([Devices & web interface](sharing.md)).
3. **GameLodge on your handheld, tablet or TV** pairs with the streaming server ([Hosts & pairing](hosts.md))
   and with GameLodge on the PC, and shows the PC's games next to its own.
